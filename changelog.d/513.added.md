Lists from a campaign's results (#513). The filter language's `enrolled_in` and `replied_in` now compile, over any of several campaigns (`campaign_ids`), and a new `campaign_outcome` predicate selects how an enrollment ended up: replied, no reply, still active, completed without reply, bounced, opted out or removed. All three combine with every other predicate in smart lists, campaign audiences, exports and bulk actions, and "none of these campaigns" is the same predicate under `not`. `replied_in` and the `replied` outcome count a response recorded by hand (#512). A campaign's page has a "Make a list from this campaign" card with one button per outcome and its count; the list is a static snapshot by default, or a live smart list, named `<campaign>: <outcome>`, and links to a new campaign built from it. The campaign builder previews how many of the chosen audience each guard would skip. The filter builder's campaign conditions are no longer greyed out.

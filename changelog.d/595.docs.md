ADR 0012 (Proposed, revised after the maintainer's review) puts the UI, LinkedIn, and setup steps such as Gmail linkage in one netkeeper Chrome: the dedicated profile with the debugging port, so you have one place to look. Your everyday Chrome can't serve, because Chrome 136 and later ignore the port on the default profile. The UI opens as an app window in netkeeper's Chrome, one launcher is the only spawn, and runs touch only their own tabs, with follow-ups #632, #633 and #634 and the Phase 7 bundled browser in #631. The research and mechanics are in `docs/design/chrome-profiles.md`.

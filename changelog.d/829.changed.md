LinkedIn and Gmail each have one automation switch: Off, Manual or Automatic (`GET /api/v1/automation`, `PUT /api/v1/automation/linkedin` and `/gmail`). LinkedIn Automatic runs quick checks (the daily incremental sync and the inbox check) or full upkeep (also the weekly full sync and the profile refresh). Gmail on Manual (armed for drafts) now checks replies only when you click Check now. Upgrading maps an armed LinkedIn schedule to Automatic with full upkeep, and an armed-and-paused or disarmed one to Manual.

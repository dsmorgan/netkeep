netkeep finds campaign emails made before the rename to netkeep again. The rename changed how netkeep derives each email's Message-ID, so a search for an email made before it missed: an email whose send was in flight across the upgrade was recorded `failed` instead of `sent` and its follow-ups stopped, a scheduled draft could be marked missing and discarded, and the Message-ID check and the leftover-drafts "Open in Gmail" link couldn't find it. Every lookup now searches for both Message-IDs; new emails keep the current one.

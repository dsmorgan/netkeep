The Dashboard's setup map is a numbered diagram: small boxes joined by right-angled arrows, the Gmail and LinkedIn tracks as two dashed groups, Skip and Undo skip on the steps you can skip (with the server's reason when it refuses), and one "Setup is complete" line once you're done. Above it, five metric tiles (Sent, Replies, Bounced, Changed jobs, New contacts) show the last 7 days, 30 days or 12 months, each with a sparkline.

Setup steps are numbered 1 to 11, with Gmail and LinkedIn as two parallel tracks: `GET /api/v1/setup` lists them in the new order with shorter titles and adds `number`, `group` and `skippable` to each step, and a "Waits for" reason names steps by number. About you and Import contacts can't be skipped, Gmail and LinkedIn can't both be skipped (409), and skipping LinkedIn sign-in skips the first sync, first inbox read and scheduled runs with it, so a Gmail-only setup can complete.

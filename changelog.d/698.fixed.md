A prefill or Message check no longer reads a contact whose profile slug starts with `login` or `authwall` as a login wall, which had flagged the session and blocked every LinkedIn job. Both now classify the landing with the slug masked, and also refuse a landing on the profile's path at another origin. The flight parser reads a stream of two or more chunks instead of refusing it as a changed route, and accepts lower-case `r` and `x` stream starts. The four LinkedIn page readers share one origin guard.

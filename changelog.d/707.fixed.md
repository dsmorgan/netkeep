**Restart netkeep** now waits for a scheduler heartbeat that is starting or finishing a scheduled run, so a restart can no longer cut a prefill or an auto-send off part way, or between Send and its record. An error while the restart waits for idle no longer leaves scheduled runs, campaign sends and the nightly backup paused: it counts as busy and the restart looks again, and **Cancel** still resumes them. Only the local owner account can restart netkeep or cancel a restart.

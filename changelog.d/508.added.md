`GET /attention` answers, in one read, what the app shell will badge: the unhandled inbox items, the LinkedIn messages and Try again steps waiting for you, the contacts waiting for triage, the draft imports, a flag for each state that blocks something (auto-send held, a mailbox that needs signing in again, the LinkedIn session flagged or heat tripped, a tripped breaker), and what LinkedIn is doing (running, stopped, paused, armed or idle). Each count matches the page that lists it. The navigation now shows it: a number on Dashboard, Triage, Imports and Inbox when items need you (capped at "99+"), and an amber dot on Dashboard, LinkedIn, Gmail and Settings when something is blocked until you deal with it. The Imports number takes you to the import history, where drafts are listed. At phone width the menu button gets a dot, and the browser tab reads "(N) netkeeper", without the Triage backlog. The dashboard says "Showing 50 of N" when more messages wait for you, or more steps wait for Try again, than it lists.

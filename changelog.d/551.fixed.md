A minimized sponsored message's bubble in LinkedIn's messaging overlay no longer stops every Message click. A `Messaging` dialog that is marked minimized and holds no composer, no header profile link, no `New message` heading and no recipient field is left out of the "already open" and "more than one bubble" counts; every other bubble still counts, and after a send the close control is looked for only in the bubble that holds the composer, so netkeeper never clicks the sponsored one.

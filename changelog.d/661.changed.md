netkeeper is now **netkeep**: the package, the command, the environment variables (`NETKEEP_*`), the UI, the data directory (`~/Library/Application Support/netkeep/`), the Keychain service (`netkeep`) and the login item (`fun.tnkr.netkeep`). The first start of the new version moves your data directory once, copies your Keychain items (the old ones stay until you delete them), and replaces your login item, keeping it on or off; it refuses, saying what to do, while netkeeper or its Chrome is still running, or when both data directories exist. `netkeep migrate --dry-run` prints the plan without changing anything, and `netkeep migrate` runs it. For one release, the `netkeeper` command, the `NETKEEPER_*` variables and the old browser cookie keep working. See [Upgrading from netkeeper](docs/guide/upgrading-from-netkeeper.md): back up first.

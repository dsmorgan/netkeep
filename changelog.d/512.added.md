You can record a response that came outside netkeeper, such as a phone call, a text, an email from another address, another app, or meeting in person. Use **Record a response** on the contact page or on a campaign's enrollment row, or run `netkeeper campaigns record-response`. It ends the contact's enrollments that could still send, as a detected reply does, so no later step sends. You can pick one campaign instead. It appears on the timeline as the contact reaching you and in the inbox already handled, and campaign results count it as a reply outside netkeeper. An opt-out ends the enrollments as opted out, and it marks the contact do not contact only if you choose that. When you log a call or meeting by hand on a contact in a live campaign, the API's answer includes `ask_if_response`, so a client can ask whether it was their response.

LinkedIn runs are recorded and can be started, watched, and stopped: `netkeep linkedin sync` and `netkeep linkedin enrich [--max-visits N] [--resume RUN]` run one by hand, `netkeep linkedin runs`, `run`, and `cancel` show and stop them, and the `/api/v1/linkedin` endpoints (runs, cancel, resume, budget, heat, pins, schedule, status) with `run.started`, `run.progress`, and `run.finished` on the event stream give the LinkedIn page the same. `netkeep serve` now runs the scheduler for the connections syncs and enrichment, but scheduled runs start disarmed on every install and fire nothing until you arm them with `netkeep linkedin schedule arm`; a browser that is unreachable or busy parks a retry 20 to 50 minutes out.

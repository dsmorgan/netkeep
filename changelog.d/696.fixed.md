Scheduled LinkedIn runs no longer stall or lose their place at the edges of active hours. A `message-check` or prefill run that was killed and left marked running no longer holds back every scheduled kind. A fire that comes due outside active hours (a long run held the scheduler past the window's end, or you narrowed the window on the Settings page) now waits for the window's next start instead of counting as run, so the daily sync, the weekly full sync and a first full sync keep their schedule. Due times pushed apart to keep kinds from running in the same minute also stay inside the window.

The web UI has a new look, direction B5 "Tinted glass" (#605): a navy sidebar, heavier headings, an electric-blue accent, and cards drawn as glass panes over a subtle color field. Cards that need you are tinted with their state color: the live run and the messages waiting to send in the accent, a paused run or a warning in amber, replies in green, and a stopped session or mailbox in red. With "Reduce transparency" on, or where the browser cannot blur, every pane is solid. Nothing the app does changed.

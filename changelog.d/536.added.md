You can switch a running campaign's LinkedIn step between Prefill and Auto-send: on the campaign page, with `netkeeper campaigns step-mode`, or through `PUT /campaigns/{id}/steps/{step_id}/mode`. Switching to Auto-send asks you to confirm first. A switch never sends anything by itself: contacts already queued for the step stay on Prefill, and only contacts who reach the step later are sent on their own, within your active hours, spacing and budget. Switching to Prefill stops any auto-send of the step that hasn't clicked Send yet. Each switch is recorded in the campaign's history (`GET /campaigns/{id}/mode-changes`), with no message text.

Gmail follow-ups now wait for replies to be read in every case. A `same_thread` follow-up is held like any other while the reply poll is behind, has not caught up since the mailbox was ready again, or was reset by a sign-in, because its own thread read misses a reply the contact sent as a fresh email. The tick now skips a held follow-up before claiming it (`replies_stale`), so the hold no longer counts as a try toward failing the step and no longer delays the mailbox's other sends by 15 minutes. The architecture doc's give-up count is corrected to 16 tries.

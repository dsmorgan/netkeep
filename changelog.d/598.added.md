LinkedIn auto-send is now a switch on the Settings page (Campaign defaults). Turning it on asks you to confirm the risks first, and the API refuses it without `confirm: true`; turning it off needs no confirmation and applies at once: an auto-send that hasn't clicked Send stops at its next check, and due steps wait for you to prefill them. Turning it on applies from the next auto-send check, with no restart. `[campaigns] linkedin_auto_send` in `config.toml` still works and wins over the page, which then shows the switch locked. Every other precondition (arming, the pause, the step's mode, the `li_messages_auto` budget, active hours, spacing, heat and the hold) still applies. `netkeeper config adopt` never moves the key.

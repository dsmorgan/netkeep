`{{ previous_send_date | ago }}` counts days in your time zone. It used the previous send's UTC day, so a follow-up a day after a 16:00 send in US Pacific winter time said "today", and one a day after a 09:00 send in Brisbane said "2 days ago"; both now say "yesterday". A bare `{{ previous_send_date }}` now renders the previous send's local date (such as `2026-11-16`) instead of its UTC timestamp. Approving a single message also lints it on your local day, as the review and the engine do, so an approval no longer passes or refuses a message on the UTC date.

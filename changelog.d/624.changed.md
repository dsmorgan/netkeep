Every attach now checks that the Chrome on the debugging port is netkeeper's dedicated profile, and refuses it otherwise (ADR 0012, rule 6). The attach reads the browser process's pid over its own connection (`SystemInfo.getProcessInfo`) and compares it with the pid in the profile's `SingletonLock`. Another Chrome on the port, such as your everyday Chrome opted in through `chrome://inspect`, or one whose profile can't be determined, is refused before netkeeper reads a context or opens a tab. `netkeeper preflight` shows a "profile" line, and the posture report has a "dedicated chrome profile" row that warns when an attach was refused.

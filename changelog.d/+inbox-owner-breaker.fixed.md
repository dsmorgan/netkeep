Scheduled LinkedIn inbox polls now stop after two polls in a row show another mailbox than your account's (`owner_mismatch`). A poll you run by hand still runs, and one that completes releases the stop. The posture report, `netkeep linkedin schedule status` and the poll-status popover name the fix: sign Chrome back in to your LinkedIn account, or run `netkeep linkedin inbox-forget-owner` when you changed accounts on purpose. If your own contact's LinkedIn ID does not match the mailbox, netkeep can't edit it yet.

`netkeeper serve` now takes the nightly backup the Nightly-backups switch promised: once a night from 03:00 in your LinkedIn timezone (or at the first check after, if the computer was asleep), keeping the newest `backup.keep` (default 14). `netkeeper restore <backup>` puts a backup back: it refuses while `serve` runs, backs up the database in place first, swaps the restored copy in atomically, and runs `db upgrade`. The Settings page lists the backups with their date, size and restore command, and has **Back up now**. The "Use the LLM" switch, which nothing read, is gone from the page.

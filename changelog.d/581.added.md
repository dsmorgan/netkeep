`netkeeper serve` now takes the nightly backup the Nightly-backups switch promised: once a night from 03:00 in your LinkedIn timezone (or within 10 minutes of waking, if the computer was asleep), keeping the newest `backup.keep` (default 14). `netkeeper restore <backup>` puts a backup back: it refuses while `serve` runs or any other process has the database open, backs up the database in place first (kept for 30 days whatever `backup.keep` says), writes the backup over it in one transaction, and runs `db upgrade`. `netkeeper db upgrade` refuses while `serve` or a restore runs. The Settings page lists the backups with their date, size and full restore command, and has **Back up now**. The "Use the LLM" switch, which nothing read, is gone from the page. A backup interrupted mid-write never shows up as one: each is written under a hidden temporary name and renamed when complete.

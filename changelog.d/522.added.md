Templates get local spelling warnings (#522). The editor's subject and body use the browser's spellcheck, and lint adds a `spelling` warning for each word the English word list and your dictionary don't know, with its line and up to three suggestions. The check is offline (`pyspellchecker`) and reads only the template's text, never a merge field, URL or email address. Your dictionary already holds your contacts' names and companies and your own name; add more with **Add to dictionary** in the editor or `netkeep templates dictionary add|list|remove`. Spelling warnings show in the editor and the campaign's activation review, and never block a save or an activation.

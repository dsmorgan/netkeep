A reply now stops every campaign the contact is in, not only the campaign whose thread it lands in. When a contact in two campaigns (or in one that sent only on LinkedIn) answered one campaign's email, the other kept sending follow-ups. The reply is recorded on each enrollment whose first send came before it; only the thread's campaign label goes on it in Gmail. An enrollment's reply time is now the earliest response on record, so a response recorded by hand that predates a detected reply credits the right step in results. The reply poll also reads less: it matches a bounce notice to a message netkeep sent by the Message-ID netkeep gave it, rather than reading every watched message's Gmail copy, and it no longer loads completed enrollments past their 30-day watch window.
